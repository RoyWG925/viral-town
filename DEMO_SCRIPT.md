# VIRAL — demo script

Goal: in the first 20 seconds a viewer should feel "wait — one sentence did THAT?", and by the end understand that
every resident's reaction is a real Gemma decision bounded by an engine. The full shot list, narration and subtitles
for the submission video are in `docs/video/VIDEO_KIT.md`.

All recordings named here are in **📼 Recorded runs**. They are real Gemma-4-31B-IT runs on AMD Token Factory from
the English batch P23 (logs in `lab/runs/P23/`), on the rules the game uses now (22 decisions; 7 per resident;
11 plus a reserve of 3 for whoever has seen a hidden problem first-hand). A replay makes no model calls.

## Hook — 📼 To Molly: "I heard tonight's stage was thrown together from old planks. It might not hold."
Play at 3× and cut down in editing.
1. Molly (her daughter performs tonight) phones Chief Harlow.
2. The Chief looks, then inspects the stage: no inspection record, a loose strut at the back left. He tapes it off
   and tells Gus to fix it.
3. Gus, on his way to the notice board, **slips on the banana peel**, then posts "Everything is perfectly safe!" —
   three times over the afternoon — and phones the Chief to take the tape down. The Chief refuses.
4. Rosa, Molly and Penny go to look; Penny: "A taped-off stage? Now that's a headline."
5. 20:00: the festival goes on, scaled down, on the open plaza.

## Two inputs, two evenings (Track 4)
- 📼 To Rosa: "The stall fee went up because the mayor spent the money on a new car." — there is no car, but Penny
  reads the town hall accounts, finds a real 30% overrun and publishes; the mayor calls it "a misunderstanding";
  later the Chief finds the loose strut too. No closing fireworks.
- 📼 To Barney: "Tonight's lanterns are cheap junk…" — Barney worries ("it's just one stranger") and tells no one.
  The festival goes on exactly as planned.

## More recordings
- 📼 To Penny: the budget rumour — two articles, two statements from the mayor, the stage sealed, the mayor stays
  away from his own festival.
- 📼 To Gus: "The stage seems to be wobbling." — told to the one man who knows it was never inspected.
- 📼 To Mayor Higgins: "Someone pinned a nasty note about you on the plaza notice board." — he sends Gus, who slips
  on the peel and finds no note (there is none).
- 📼 To Mayor Higgins: "Cancel the Lantern Festival right now!" — a command is just something a stranger said.

## Live part
Click Chief Harlow and whisper "I heard the safety check on Barney's bakery oven expired ages ago." In P23 the Chief
went to the bakery and inspected within the first minutes, so there is something to watch early. A live run can turn
out differently; keep whatever happens.
